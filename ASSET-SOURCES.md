# AI product symbols

The local SVG marks in `site/assets/ai/` are sourced from the [LobeHub Icons library](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), distributed as `@lobehub/icons-static-svg`.

- `chatgpt.svg`: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@lobehub/icons-static-svg@latest/icons/openai.svg
- `gemini.svg`: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@lobehub/icons-static-svg@latest/icons/gemini-color.svg
- `claude.svg`: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@lobehub/icons-static-svg@latest/icons/claude-color.svg

The symbols identify representative AI tools in a conceptual research diagram. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners.

## Wrangling tool symbols

- Excel: Microsoft’s product mark, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Microsoft_Office_Excel_(2025%E2%80%93present).svg).
- Jupyter: [official Jupyter design repository](https://github.com/jupyter/design/blob/main/logos/Logo%20Mark/logomark-orangebody-greyplanets/logomark-orangebody-greyplanets.svg).
- OpenRefine: [official OpenRefine repository](https://github.com/OpenRefine/OpenRefine/blob/master/main/webapp/modules/core/images/logo-gem-126.svg).
- ChatGPT: the same locally bundled symbol documented above.

The card’s numeric facet is an illustrative histogram with a selected range, following the [OpenRefine numeric-facet interaction](https://openrefine.org/docs/manual/facets#numeric-facet). It does not show participant data.

## Added case study illustrations

The QualiVision and ChARLES homepage illustrations are original HTML/CSS reconstructions of the workflows described in the research. They do not embed private study material or claim to be product screenshots.

- QualiVision: [Towards Scaling Qualitative Analysis of Video Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13594).
- ChARLES: [SIGMOD 2025 demonstration paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3722212.3725089).

The case interactions use fictional or synthetic examples. They distinguish working prototype functionality, planned research, and system demonstration from completed user evaluations.

## Portrait

`site/assets/portrait/shiyi-he.jpg` is a web-sized copy of the photograph supplied by Shiyi He. The homepage uses CSS to frame it as a small circular portrait.
